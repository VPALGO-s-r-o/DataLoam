# Support

- **Documentation**: [dataloam.io/docs](https://www.dataloam.io/docs)
- **Bugs and feature requests**: [open an issue](https://github.com/VPALGO-s-r-o/DataLoam/issues/new/choose) using one of the forms
- **Questions, account, and billing**: email [support@dataloam.io](mailto:support@dataloam.io)
- **Security vulnerabilities**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

You can also open the issue tracker from your editor with the **Report an Issue** button in the DataLoam Account view.
